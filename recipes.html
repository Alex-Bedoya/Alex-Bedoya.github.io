01Weeknight favorite

Lemon & garlic pasta

Lemon, garlic, and Parmesan.

Serves 2 · 20 minutes

Open recipe

Ingredients

  • 200 g pasta
  • 2 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1 lemon (zest and juice)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Parmesan, black pepper, and salt

Method

  1. Cook the pasta in salted water. Save a mug of pasta water before draining.
  2. Warm the olive oil and garlic gently until fragrant. Stir in lemon zest.
  3. Toss in the pasta, lemon juice, and a splash of pasta water. Finish with Parmesan and pepper.
02One-pot comfort

Tomato chickpea stew

Chickpeas, tomato, and warm spices.

Serves 4 · 35 minutes

Open recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cans chickpeas, drained
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp cumin and 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Olive oil, salt, and a handful of greens

Method

  1. Soften the onion in olive oil. Stir in the spices for 30 seconds.
  2. Add chickpeas, tomatoes, and a splash of water. Simmer for 25 minutes.
  3. Fold in the greens until wilted. Season to taste and serve with crusty bread.
03Make-ahead lunch

Roasted veg & tahini bowls

Roasted vegetables with tahini sauce.

Serves 3–4 · 40 minutes

Open recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 sweet potato and 1 small cauliflower, chopped
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, cumin, salt, and pepper
  • 3 tbsp tahini and 1 lemon
  • Cooked rice or grain, to serve

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C / 425°F. Toss the vegetables and chickpeas with oil and seasoning.
  2. Roast on a sheet pan for 25–30 minutes, turning once.
  3. Whisk tahini with lemon juice and enough water to make a pourable sauce. Serve over grains.
04Slow morning

Saturday pancakes

Buttermilk pancakes.

Makes 8 · 25 minutes

Open recipe

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp baking powder, ½ tsp baking soda
  • 1 egg and 1¼ cups buttermilk
  • 3 tbsp melted butter, plus more for the pan
  • Maple syrup and fruit, to serve

Method

  1. Whisk the dry ingredients in a bowl. In another bowl, whisk together egg, buttermilk, and butter.
  2. Fold wet into dry just until combined; a few lumps are fine.
  3. Cook small scoops in a buttered pan over medium heat. Flip when bubbles appear. Serve warm.
