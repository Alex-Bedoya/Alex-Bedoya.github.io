About me

Born and raised in Utah. Curious by nature, I love learning, exploring, and trying new things. Whether I'm cooking, snowboarding, mountain biking, or getting lost in conversations about books, philosophy, and movies, I'm always looking for new experiences and ways to make the most of life.

Background

Work, Leisure, Activism

I graduated from Weber State University with a degree in Computer Science and currently work as a Software Engineer and Data Engineer at Hill Air Force Base.

Outside of work, I enjoy staying active, exploring the city, and finding new experiences. You'll often find me spending time with friends, catching a movie, going to concerts, exploring local bars, snowboarding, mountain biking, or getting out with my camera. I'm also a fan of jazz, photography, farmers markets, flea markets, and local art shows.

I enjoy being involved in the community, too, whether that's attending local events, supporting charity initiatives, showing up for Pride, or participating in protests. I'm always looking for something new to experience, learn, or get involved in.
